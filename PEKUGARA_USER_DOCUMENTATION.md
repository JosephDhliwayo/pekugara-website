## 1. What is Pekugara?

Pekugara is a dedicated student housing platform built to connect university and college students in Zimbabwe with verified landlords offering affordable, nearby accommodation. The name *Pekugara* comes from the Shona word meaning *"a place to stay"* — and that is exactly what the platform delivers.

Whether you are a student searching for a safe, affordable room close to campus, or a landlord looking to fill your property with reliable tenants, Pekugara provides a trusted, transparent, and easy-to-use environment to make that connection happen.

---

## 2. Why Use Pekugara?

### For Students

Finding student accommodation in Zimbabwe has traditionally been stressful, unreliable, and unsafe. Students often rely on word of mouth, social media groups, or unverified agents — leading to fraud, unsafe living conditions, and wasted money. Pekugara solves this by:

- **Verified listings only** — every property is reviewed and approved by the Pekugara moderation team before appearing in the feed. No fake listings.
- **Verified landlords** — landlords can earn a verified badge after identity review, giving students confidence in who they are dealing with.
- **Transparent pricing** — rent prices are always displayed upfront. No hidden fees, no surprises.
- **Direct communication** — students message landlords directly through the in-app chat. No third-party agents, no commissions.
- **Safe environment** — a built-in reporting and blocking system protects students from harassment or fraud, with a moderation team that responds to every report.
- **Campus proximity** — listings display their distance from your university, making it easy to find accommodation within walking distance.

### For Landlords

Managing student tenants and marketing your property can be time-consuming and expensive. Pekugara makes it simple by:

- **Free listings** — post your property to hundreds of students actively searching for housing, at no cost.
- **Organised bookings** — all viewing requests and tenant confirmations are managed in one place, with real-time notifications.
- **Pre-qualified leads** — students pay a small booking fee to confirm serious interest, reducing time-wasters.
- **Verified badge** — get a verification badge that increases student trust and improves your listing visibility.
- **Direct messaging** — communicate with interested students without giving out personal contact details prematurely.
- **Occupancy tracking** — see at a glance how many spots are filled and how many are still available for each property.

## 3. Getting Started

### 3.1 Creating an Account

1. Open the Pekugara app.
2. On the Welcome screen, tap:
   - **"Find a Room"** — to register as a renter (students can optionally add their university for campus-distance search).
   - **"List your property"** — to register as a landlord.
   - **"I already have an account"** — to log in.
3. Fill in your details:
   - **Students:** Full name, email, password, and university/college.
   - **Landlords:** Full name, email, password, and phone number.
4. Read and accept the **Terms of Service and EULA**.
5. Tap **"Create Account"**.

> Your account is created instantly. Students can browse listings immediately. Landlords can post listings immediately, but listings require admin approval before going live.

---

### 3.2 Logging In

1. Tap **"I already have an account"** on the Welcome screen.
2. Enter your registered **email** and **password**.
3. Tap **"Sign In"**.

> If your account has been suspended, you will be notified on login and will not be able to access the app.

---

### 3.3 Account Verification

While any user can create an account, getting **verified** adds a visible badge to your profile and increases trust with other users.

- **Students:** Submit a verification request from Profile → Account Verification. Provide supporting details (e.g., student ID description).
- **Landlords:** Submit a verification request from Profile → Account Verification. An admin will review your property and identity details.

Once approved, a green **Verified** badge will appear on your profile and listings.

## 4. For Students

### 4.1 Browsing the Home Feed

After logging in, students land on the **Home** screen which shows all available, admin-approved listings.

- The feed automatically **excludes your own listings** (if any) and **excludes listings from users you have blocked**.
- Use the **search bar** at the top to search by city, campus name, or area.
- Use the **filter chips** below the search bar to quickly filter by:
  - **Near Campus** — listings within 1 km of a university.
  - **Furnished** — fully furnished rooms.
  - **WiFi** — listings with internet included.
  - **Ensuite** — listings with a private bathroom.
- Pull down on the feed to **refresh** listings.

---

### 4.2 Advanced Search

Tap the **Search** tab in the bottom navigation for more powerful filtering options.

1. Type a search keyword (city, campus, area).
2. Tap the **filter icon** to open the filter panel:
   - **Price Range** — drag the slider to set a maximum monthly rent.
   - **City** — select from Harare, Bulawayo, Mutare, Gweru, Masvingo.
   - **University** — filter by nearest university (UZ, MSU, NUST, Midlands State, Africa University).
   - **Amenities** — toggle Furnished, WiFi, Ensuite, Parking, Utilities Included.
   - **Sort By** — Newest, Price Low to High, Price High to Low, Closest to Campus.
3. Tap **Apply** to see filtered results.
4. Tap **Reset** to clear all filters.

---

### 4.3 Saving Listings

- Tap the **heart icon** on any listing card to save it.
- Access all your saved listings from the **Saved** tab in the bottom navigation.
- Tap a saved listing to open its full details.
- Tap the heart again on any listing to unsave it.

---

### 4.4 Viewing a Listing in Detail

Tap any listing card to open the full **Detail** screen.

**What you will see:**
- Full-size image gallery — swipe left and right to browse all photos.
- Property title, address, city, and distance to campus.
- Bedroom count, bathroom count, and maximum tenant capacity.
- Monthly rent price.
- Full description from the landlord.
- Amenities grid showing which features are included.
- Landlord card showing their name and verification status.

**Actions available:**
- Save or unsave the listing.
- Report the listing if you believe it is fraudulent or inappropriate.
- Proceed to book a viewing (see Section 4.5).

---

### 4.5 Booking a Viewing

To request a viewing with a landlord, students are required to pay a **$2 USD booking fee** via EcoCash. This fee confirms your serious interest and protects landlords from time-wasters.

**Steps to book:**

1. On the Detail screen, tap **"Pay Booking Fee — $2"**.
2. Enter your **EcoCash phone number**.
3. Tap **"Send Payment Request"**.
4. A payment prompt will be sent to your phone — approve it on your device.
5. The app will automatically confirm your payment (within 3 minutes).
6. Once confirmed, tap **"Request a Viewing"**.
7. Write a short message to the landlord (e.g., when you are available, any questions).
8. Tap **"Send Request"**.

> Your booking request is sent to the landlord. You will receive an in-app notification when the landlord confirms or rejects your request.

**Note:** The booking fee is a one-time fee that unlocks viewing requests across all listings. You only pay it once.

---

### 4.6 After Your Booking is Confirmed

Once a landlord confirms your booking:

- The Home screen switches to a **"Housing Confirmed"** view, showing your confirmed property details.
- You can see your landlord's name and phone number.
- You can message your landlord directly from this screen.
- You will no longer see the general listings feed — your housing journey is complete.

If a landlord rejects your request, you will be notified and can request a viewing at a different property.

---

### 4.7 Messaging Landlords

Once you have paid the booking fee, you can open a chat with any landlord from the Detail screen.

- Tap **"Message Landlord"** or the chat button on the landlord card.
- The **Chat** screen opens with a preview of the listing at the top.
- Type your message and tap **Send**.
- Messages are delivered in real time.

> Messages are monitored for community guideline violations. Offensive or abusive content will be blocked before sending.

All your conversations are accessible from the **Chats** tab in the bottom navigation.

---

### 4.8 Siyakwamukela AI — Your Housing Assistant

Not sure where to start? Chat with **Siyakwamukela AI**, Pekugara's built-in AI housing assistant. Describe what you are looking for — budget, city, university, amenities — and it will recommend listings that match, based on your conversation.

> Your messages are only used to generate recommendations during your session and are never used to train AI models. See our [Privacy Policy](https://pekugara.com/privacy-policy) for details.

---

### 4.9 Viewing Your Booking Requests

Go to **Profile → My Viewing Requests** to see all the requests you have submitted and their current status:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | The landlord has not yet responded. |
| **Confirmed** | The landlord has accepted your request. |
| **Rejected** | The landlord has declined your request. |
| **Cancelled** | You or the landlord cancelled the request. |

You can **cancel** a pending request at any time by tapping **Cancel** on the request card.

### 4.10 House & Room Sharing

Already have a place but need a roommate to split rent with? Open the **Sharing** tab to post your own ad or browse ads from others.

- If you have a confirmed booking through Pekugara, your sharing ad is **verified automatically** and goes live immediately.
- If your place isn't booked through Pekugara, you can still post — your ad is reviewed by our team before it becomes visible to others.

Messaging a sharing ad poster requires the same $2 booking fee as messaging a landlord — one payment unlocks both for 30 days.

## 5. For Landlords

### 5.1 The Landlord Dashboard

After logging in, landlords see a **Dashboard** on the Home screen showing:

- **Listings** — total number of active properties posted.
- **Confirmed** — total number of confirmed student bookings.
- **Pending** — total number of pending viewing requests awaiting your response.

Quick action cards let you navigate directly to **My Listings** or **Add New Listing**.

---

### 5.2 Adding a New Listing

Tap **"Add new listing"** from the dashboard or the **+** button on My Listings.

**Fill in the listing form:**

**Basic Information**
- Property title (e.g., "Furnished Room Near UZ Campus")
- Description — describe the property, nearby landmarks, and any house rules.
- Monthly rent in USD.

**Location**
- City
- Full street address
- Nearest university or college
- Distance from that university (in km)

**Rooms**
- Number of bedrooms
- Number of bathrooms
- Maximum number of students the property can accommodate

**Amenities** (toggle on/off)
- Furnished
- Ensuite bathroom
- WiFi included
- Parking available
- Utilities included

**Photos**
- Add a minimum of **5 photos** and a maximum of **10 photos**.
- The first photo is automatically set as the **cover image**.
- Photos should clearly show the rooms, bathroom, kitchen, and exterior.

Once complete, tap **"Publish Listing"**. Your listing will be submitted for admin review. Once approved, it will go live and be visible to all students.

> Listings that do not meet community standards (e.g., misleading photos, false descriptions) will be rejected by the admin team.

---

### 5.3 Managing Your Listings

Go to the **My Listings** screen to manage all your posted properties.

**For each listing you can see:**
- Property title, city, and monthly price.
- Occupancy bar showing how many spots are filled out of the maximum.
- Confirmed students, pending requests, and remaining spots.
- A list of all booking requests — tap a listing card to expand it.

**Responding to booking requests:**

| Action | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Confirm** | Student is marked as housed. They receive a "Confirmed" notification. The student's feed is locked to your property. |
| **Reject** | Request is declined. Student receives a rejection notification and can apply elsewhere. |
| **Remove** | Remove a previously confirmed student (e.g., they moved out). Student is notified that housing was cancelled. |

> When you confirm a student, they receive an instant in-app notification and push notification. Real-time updates mean students see their status change immediately.

---

### 5.4 Getting Verified

A **Verified badge** on your profile and listings significantly increases student trust.

To apply:
1. Go to **Profile → Account Verification**.
2. Tap **"Request Verification"**.
3. Enter supporting notes (e.g., property ownership details, registration info).
4. Submit your request.

An admin will review your submission. You will be notified of the outcome. If rejected, the reason will be shown and you may reapply.

## 6. Safety & Moderation

Pekugara takes the safety of its community seriously. The following features are in place to protect all users.

### 6.1 Content Moderation

- All listings are **reviewed by a human admin** before going live. No unverified listing is ever shown to students.
- The in-app chat has a built-in **offensive language filter** that blocks messages containing abusive or inappropriate content before they are sent.

### 6.2 Reporting a User or Listing

If you encounter abusive behaviour, a suspicious listing, or inappropriate content:

**To report a listing:**
- Open the listing Detail screen.
- Tap the **flag icon** in the top-right corner.
- Select a reason: Inappropriate Content, Spam/Misleading, Fraud/Scam, Harassment/Abuse, or Other.
- Optionally add a description.
- Tap **Submit Report**.

**To report a user from chat:**
- Open the chat with that user.
- Tap the **⋮ menu** in the top-right corner.
- Tap **"Report [Name]"**.
- Select a reason and submit.

All reports are sent to the Pekugara moderation team and reviewed within 24 hours. If a report is substantiated, the reported user may be suspended or permanently removed from the platform.

> False reports made in bad faith may result in action against the reporting account.

### 6.3 Blocking a User

If a user is harassing you or behaving inappropriately, you can **block** them directly.

**To block a user:**
1. Open your chat with that user.
2. Tap the **⋮ menu** in the top-right corner.
3. Tap **"Block [Name]"**.
4. Confirm by tapping **"Block"** in the prompt.

**What happens when you block someone:**
- Their listings are **immediately removed** from your Home feed.
- An automatic report is filed with the moderation team.
- The moderation team is notified instantly.
- You are returned to the Home screen where the blocked user's content is no longer visible.

### 6.4 Managing Blocked Users

To view or unblock users you have previously blocked:

1. Go to **Profile**.
2. Scroll to the **Safety** section.
3. Tap **"Blocked users"**.
4. Tap **"Unblock"** next to any user to remove the block.

## 7. Account & Profile Management

### 7.1 Editing Your Profile

1. Go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap **"Edit"** next to Personal Info.
3. Update your:
   - Full name
   - University (students)
   - Phone number
   - (Email is fixed and cannot be changed)
4. Tap **"Save"** to apply changes.

### 7.2 Logging Out

1. Go to **Profile**.
2. Tap the **logout icon** (arrow pointing out) in the top-right corner.
3. Confirm by tapping **"Log out"**.

### 7.3 Deleting Your Account

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data — listings, bookings, messages, and profile — will be erased.

1. Go to **Profile**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **"Delete Account"**.
3. Confirm twice in the prompts that appear.

## 8. Notifications

Pekugara sends both **in-app notifications** and **push notifications** to keep you informed in real time.

### Notification Types

| Notification | Who receives it | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| New booking request | Landlord | A student submitted a viewing request. |
| Booking confirmed | Student | A landlord accepted your viewing request. |
| Booking rejected | Student | A landlord declined your viewing request. |
| Booking cancelled | Student | Your confirmed housing was cancelled. |
| User blocked (report) | Admin team | A user block was reported to moderation. |

Access all notifications from **Profile → the bell icon** in the top-right corner. All notifications are marked as read automatically when you open the Notifications screen.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is Pekugara free to use?**
A: Yes. Creating an account, browsing listings, saving listings, and messaging landlords are all free. Students pay a one-time $2 USD booking fee via EcoCash to unlock viewing requests.

---

**Q: Why do I need to pay a $2 booking fee?**
A: The booking fee confirms that you are a serious student looking for accommodation and not a time-waster. It is a one-time fee and unlocks viewing requests across all listings on the platform.

---

**Q: How do I know a listing is genuine?**
A: Every listing on Pekugara is manually reviewed and approved by our moderation team before it appears in the feed. Listings with false or misleading information are rejected. You can also check whether the landlord has a **Verified** badge on their profile.

---

**Q: I paid the booking fee but the listing was taken. Can I get a refund?**
A: The $2 booking fee is not tied to a specific listing — it unlocks the ability to send viewing requests across the entire platform. It is not refundable once paid.

---

**Q: Can a landlord see my contact details?**
A: Your profile shows your full name and university (students) or phone number (landlords) to the other party once a booking request is made. All communication before that happens securely within the in-app chat.

---

**Q: What happens if a landlord never responds to my request?**
A: You can cancel a pending request at any time from Profile → My Viewing Requests, and send a request to a different property. If a landlord is consistently unresponsive, you may report them.

---

**Q: I am a landlord. Does it cost anything to list my property?**
A: No. Posting listings on Pekugara is completely free for landlords.

---

**Q: How long does admin approval take for a new listing?**
A: Listings are typically reviewed within 24 hours. You will receive an in-app notification once your listing is approved and live.

---

**Q: What should I do if I experience harassment?**
A: Block the user immediately from the chat screen (⋮ menu → Block). This removes them from your feed and automatically notifies our moderation team, who will review the case within 24 hours.

---

**Q: Can I use Pekugara if I am not a student?**
A: Yes. Pekugara is open to any renter, not just students — the "I'm a student" option during sign-up is optional and only used to unlock campus-distance search.

---

**Q: How does House & Room Sharing work?**
A: If you already have accommodation and want to split rent, post a sharing ad from the Sharing tab. Ads from renters with a confirmed Pekugara booking go live instantly; ads from anyone else are reviewed by our team first. Messaging a sharing ad poster uses the same $2 booking fee as messaging a landlord.
