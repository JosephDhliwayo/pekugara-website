This Privacy Policy explains how Pekugara ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information from users of the Pekugara mobile application and website (collectively, the "Platform"). By using Pekugara, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use Pekugara:

  • Account information: Your full name, email address, phone number, role (renter or landlord), and university or college affiliation if you're a student (optional, used to unlock campus-distance search).
  • Profile information: Profile photo (uploaded from your camera or photo library), verification documents submitted for identity or landlord verification, and any updates you make to your profile.
  • Listing information (landlords): Property details, photos, location coordinates, pricing, and amenity information submitted when creating a listing.
  • Location data: Approximate or precise location when you use map features, search for a location by name, or when a landlord pins a property location.
  • Payment information: EcoCash phone number and transaction reference numbers used to process booking fees. We do not store full payment card details.
  • Biometric login: If you enable Face ID or fingerprint login, this is handled entirely by your device's operating system — we never receive, see, or store your biometric data.
  • Messages: Chat messages sent between renters and landlords through the in-app messaging system, including house and room sharing conversations.
  • Usage data: Information about how you interact with the app, including searches, listings viewed, bookings made, and features used.
  • Device information: Device type, operating system, and push notification token used to deliver notifications to your device.
2

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account on the Pekugara platform.
  • Display verified property listings to renters searching for accommodation.
  • Facilitate communication between renters and landlords through in-app messaging.
  • Process booking fee payments securely through our payment partner Pesepay.
  • Send push notifications and in-app alerts about bookings, messages, and account activity.
  • Review and approve or reject listing submissions and verification requests.
  • Investigate reports of suspicious or harmful activity and enforce our community standards.
  • Improve the performance, features, and safety of the Pekugara platform.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
3

Siyakwamukela AI

Pekugara includes an AI-powered housing assistant called Siyakwamukela AI. When you use this feature, the messages you send and the conversation history from your current session are processed to generate accommodation recommendations. These messages are sent to our AI service provider to generate responses.

We do not use your AI conversations to train AI models.
4

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Between users: When a renter requests a viewing or messages about a sharing ad, their name and message are shared with the relevant landlord or poster. Landlord names, phone numbers, and property details are visible to renters who have paid the $2 booking fee.
  • Service providers: We share necessary data with Supabase (database and authentication), Pesepay (payment processing), and our AI service provider (Siyakwamukela AI feature) to operate the platform.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger or acquisition, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
5

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase infrastructure. We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), row-level security policies on our database, and access controls that restrict who can view and modify user data. While we take security seriously, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6

Data Retention

We retain your account information and activity data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data from our systems within 30 days, except where retention is required by law. Some information may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period.

7

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Update or correct inaccurate information through your profile settings.
  • Delete your account and associated personal data through the app's account settings.
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing where applicable.
  • Lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority in your jurisdiction.
8

Age Restriction

Pekugara is intended for renters and landlords aged 18 and above, consistent with our Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has registered, please contact us so we can remove their information.

9

Third-Party Links and Services

The Pekugara app integrates with Google Maps and Apple Maps for location features. Your use of these mapping features is also subject to Google's and Apple's respective privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify users through the app or by email. Your continued use of Pekugara after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. The date at the top of this page reflects when the policy was last revised.
